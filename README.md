# Atoosa Biglari — Enterprise TPM & AI Operations

A public, interactive portfolio connecting three parts of one enterprise story:

1. **Who I Am** — Enterprise TPM capabilities, experience, delivery approach, and program control system.
2. **Vision & Innovation** — A proposed AI Operations model grounded in facts, human authority, bounded action, and measurable outcomes.
3. **Corgi Cafe Nexus** — A working showcase of connected frontline, kitchen, inventory, workforce, finance, and executive operations.

## Public experience

- Portfolio home: `https://atoosabiglari-pm.github.io/enterprise-tpm-corgi-cafe-showcase/`
- Vision & Innovation: `https://atoosabiglari-pm.github.io/enterprise-tpm-corgi-cafe-showcase/vision/`
- Corgi Cafe Nexus: `https://atoosabiglari-pm.github.io/enterprise-tpm-corgi-cafe-showcase/nexus/`

## Purpose

This showcase demonstrates how Atoosa Biglari connects enterprise Technical Program Management, transformation, migration, AI Operations, and real frontline workflows into a governed operating model.

The Corgi Cafe material is a proposed innovation showcase. Existing company systems and policies remain authoritative.
