Responsible Autonomy

AI earns permission; it does not assume it.

Every use case begins at the lowest useful level. More autonomy is granted only when evidence, controls, ownership, and reversibility are strong enough.

01

Observe

AI sees facts; humans act.

02

Assist

AI organizes and summarizes.

03

Recommend

AI proposes; a person decides.

04

Execute with Approval

AI prepares action; authorized human releases it.

05

Bounded Autonomy

AI acts only inside explicit limits, with monitoring and reversal.
